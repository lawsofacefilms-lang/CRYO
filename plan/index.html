04  What happens next

The same four steps, every month.

  1. Pre. Bryan replies to confirm the day. The questions and any ad script are written from what you sent and go to you and your advertising team for review.

  2. Film. The production day. Once filming is complete, editing begins.

  3. Post. Everything from that day is built.

  4. Delivery. One link, your feedback, and this page again for the next month.

If a patient falls through, the day still happens. It switches to a Health Day or a Practice Day and the patient moves to the following month.
