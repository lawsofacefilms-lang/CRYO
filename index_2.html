That is the single most useful thing said in the meeting, and it reorders everything. Social content is real, but it is not where most of the money is being decided. The service pages are. So the service pages lead this plan, and every other piece of content is built to feed them.

There is also a constraint worth naming up front. Meta will not carry guaranteed outcomes, transformation imagery, or language that names a problem with someone's body. That rules out most of what the aesthetics industry advertises with. It does not rule out the practice itself, which is the more durable thing to sell anyway: the science, the individualized plan, and the person delivering it.

How a production day is organized

By who is on camera, not by video. Every question is tagged in advance to where the answer is going, which is what allows one day to feed several deliverables.

  1. Dr. Poe, part one. Educational topics, in her own framing, for organic.

  2. Dr. Poe, part two. Service by service explanations that anchor each website film.

  3. Dr. Poe, part three. Short, ad-ready answers written to platform guidelines.

  4. Patients. Overall experience first, then the specific service they received.

Facility and treatment footage is captured the same day where the schedule allows. Everything is shot horizontal so vertical versions come out of the same take rather than a second setup.

04  Sample interview questions

The questions that build the films.

A starting point, so the approach is visible before anything is shot. Final questions are written and sent for review ahead of each shoot day.

For Dr. Poe

Educational
  • What are the most common concerns people bring to you before they know what service they need?
  • What do people most often misunderstand about non-invasive body contouring?
  • How do you help someone figure out what is right for their individual goals?
  • What does a personalized plan actually mean here, in practice?
Service explanation
  • Walk me through what this service is, in plain language.
  • Who tends to be a good fit for it, and how do you decide?
  • What does the experience look like from the moment someone walks in?
  • What does the science behind it actually do?
Authority and care
  • What made you decide to bring this technology into the practice?
  • What do you want someone to know about the level of care here before they ever book?
  • What does restoring someone's health mean to you, beyond the treatment itself?

For patients

Overall experience
  • What made you decide to come to Cryo & Contour?
  • What has the experience with Dr. Poe and the team been like?
  • What would you tell someone who is considering coming in?
The specific service
  • What drew you to this particular service?
  • What was the process like for you, start to finish?
  • What surprised you about it?

Answers are guided so the ad cuts can be assembled without language that paid placement will not carry.
